> First scene adaptation from Act 1, Chapter 1 of "แสงที่ความยาวคลื่นสิบสามจุดห้า"
> Format optimized for TikTok / Reels / Shorts
> Total: 11 shots + title/end card ≈ 60 seconds

## Editing notes

- **Cut on action** between Shot 4→5 (the corridor turn reveals the machine)
- **J-cut** Wang's voice in early at Shot 7's end (we hear "เพราะมันแก่..." before we see him speak in Shot 8)
- **Hold longer** on Shot 11 — the mom message is the emotional landing
- **Music swell** starts at Shot 8 (mentor's line), peaks at Shot 9b (wide cathedral shot), drops at Shot 10
- **Silence** for 1 full second after voiceover "หนูสบายดี" before end card

---

## Seedance generation tips

1. **Generate 3 variants** per shot — pick the best, throw out 2
2. **Image-to-video** for consistency: generate one good still per character first, then animate
3. **Negative prompts** to add: "blurry, watermark, distorted faces, text overlay, oversaturated, plastic skin"
4. **Seed** — reuse seed for shots with the same character (Wenli, Wang) to keep face consistent
5. **Don't over-prompt** — Seedance does best with 30-50 words per shot; cut adjectives if needed
